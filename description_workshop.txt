[b] The Asphalt Mod [/b] is a small content mod that adds multiple items related to asphalt.
It currently adds:
[list]
[*] Melee, ranged, and magic weapons
[*] A complete set of tools
[*] Asphalt boots accessory
[*] Asphalt movement speed and weapon speed potions
[*] Asphalt Car mount
[*] Asphalt grappling hook
[*] Asphalt platforms
[*] Asphalt rope
[*] Dense Asphalt (asphalt upgrade)
[*] Asphalt Slime enemy
[*] Asphalt furniture, bricks and walls
[/list]

This mod started being developed in early 2023, as part of the asphalt meme trend going on at the time, but I started taking it more seriously over time, and now it should be decently fit enough into the game.

Mod developed by mimig298.
